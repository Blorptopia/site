What does turnuva do

  • Keeps track of scores per match
  • "Bucket" matchmaking where everyone in the bucket plays against eachother
  • Bracket matchmaking, where you play until defeated.
  • Visualizes the entire process so it's transparent for the players

History

Turnuva was initially a Svelte-Kit app, however it was used as our test-project for our migration over to Lit.
